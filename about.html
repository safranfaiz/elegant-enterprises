Our story

Made for everyday hygiene

Elegant Enterprises Pvt Ltd was built to make essential sanitary and personal care products practical to source in Southern Sri Lanka. We manufacture a focused range — tissues, masks, baby care, and household hygiene — so partners can buy locally instead of waiting on long import lead times.

We work with distributors, shops, hotels, clinics, and corporate buyers who need consistent quality, clear labelling, and a team that answers the phone.
