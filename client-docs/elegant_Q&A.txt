# 1. What is Elegant Enterprises?

*Elegant Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd* is a Sri Lankan manufacturing and distribution company specialising in paper tissue products, hygiene products and protective face masks.

We are committed to delivering quality products at competitive prices while maintaining high standards of hygiene, reliability and customer satisfaction.

Our focus is on providing dependable products and building long-term business relationships with wholesalers, distributors, retailers, hotels, restaurants, supermarkets and other corporate customers across Sri Lanka.

With a commitment to quality, affordability and continuous improvement, we aim to become a trusted name in Sri Lanka's tissue and hygiene product industry.

# 2. What Products Do We Manufacture?

At Elegant Enterprises, we manufacture a range of paper tissue and hygiene products to meet the everyday needs of households, businesses and institutions.
