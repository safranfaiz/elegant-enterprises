Our plant

Factory Details

Production, packing, and dispatch for hygiene products — on Akuressa Road, Galle.

Facility

How the plant is organised

The factory has dedicated areas for raw-material storage, converting and assembly, packaging, and finished-goods dispatch. Paper and non-woven work are kept apart where required, and packing zones stay clean.

Production capabilities

  • Tissue converting — rolls, serviettes, and related paper goods
  • Mask production and packing — disposable and respirator formats
  • Bulk packing, carton labelling, and pallet-ready dispatch
  • Custom runs for qualified B2B partners (minimum order applies)

Quality & safety

We follow good manufacturing practice suited to our categories: staff hygiene in production areas, batch traceability, and a final visual check before cartons are sealed. Fire exits and equipment care are reviewed on a regular schedule.
