Company

About Elegant Enterprises

What is Elegant Enterprises?
Elegant Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan manufacturing and distribution company based in Galle. We specialise in paper tissue products and protective face masks, with a focus on quality, competitive wholesale pricing, and reliable supply for trade buyers across Sri Lanka. Learn more on our About Us page.
Who can buy from Elegant Enterprises?
We welcome wholesalers, distributors, retailers, supermarkets, hotels, restaurants, hospitals, institutions and other bulk buyers throughout Sri Lanka. Retail shops and resellers may purchase for their local markets.
Who do you supply across Sri Lanka?
We supply distributors, supermarkets, pharmacies, hotels, clinics, schools and corporate buyers island-wide. Orders ship from our Galle factory in retail packs or bulk cartons.

Products

What we manufacture

What tissue and mask products do you make?
We manufacture paper serviettes / paper napkins, toilet tissue rolls, multifold hand towels, kitchen paper towels, 3-ply disposable face masks, KN95 face masks, and other protective face masks. See product details for pack formats.
Can I buy at wholesale prices?
Yes. Wholesale purchasing is available with competitive rates for bulk orders. Share your product list and monthly volumes so we can quote trade pricing and carton sizes.

Ordering

Wholesale & delivery

How can buyers work with us?
Buyers can purchase wholesale, become distribution partners, arrange hotel and corporate supply, buy through retail and reseller partnerships, or request custom bulk orders. See what we can do for partnership options.
How do you place an order with Elegant Enterprises?
  1. Contact sales by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  2. Share required products and quantities.
  3. Receive product prices and a quotation.
  4. Confirm your order.
  5. Arrange delivery and payment with our team.
Do you sell tissue and face masks at wholesale prices in Sri Lanka?
Yes. We are the manufacturer, so shops, distributors, hotels and offices buy directly from our Galle factory at wholesale prices with no middleman. Prices depend on product, pack size and order quantity.
What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order depends on the product and pack format. Call or WhatsApp 074 100 8160 with the products and quantities you need and we will confirm pricing and minimums.
Do you deliver outside Galle?
Yes. We supply buyers across Sri Lanka, with short lead times in the Southern Province. Delivery options are confirmed with each quotation.
Can I get products under my own brand?
Yes. We produce private-label runs of paper tissue and face masks for qualified trade partners. See the private label section below for more detail.
How do I request a wholesale quote?
Use the contact form, email sales@elegantenterprises.lk or WhatsApp 074 100 8160 with product names, pack sizes and monthly volumes. We reply with pricing and lead times.

Private label

OEM & your brand

Do you offer private label manufacturing?
Yes. Private label means making and packing hygiene products under your brand at our plant. You share specs and artwork; we run production, quality checks and dispatch on agreed volumes.
Can Elegant Enterprises manufacture tissue or masks under my own brand?
Yes. We offer private label and OEM manufacturing of paper tissue and face masks at our factory in Galle. You supply the brand and we handle production, packing and labelling.
What is the minimum order quantity for private label manufacturing?
Minimum order quantities depend on the product, pack size and packaging. Share your product mix and expected volumes and we will confirm the MOQ, pricing and lead time in your quotation.
Can I visit the factory before placing an order?
Yes. Trade buyers can visit our plant at 496 Akuressa Road, Galle by appointment. Call or WhatsApp 074 100 8160 to arrange a visit. See factory details.
