What is Elegant Enterprises?

Elegant Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan manufacturing and distribution company. We make paper tissue, hygiene products, and protective face masks. We focus on quality, fair wholesale pricing, and reliable supply for trade buyers.

We serve wholesalers, distributors, retailers, hotels, restaurants, supermarkets, hospitals, and corporate customers across Sri Lanka. Our Akuressa Road factory in Galle runs converting, packing, and dispatch for island-wide orders.

Private label manufacturing is when we produce and pack hygiene goods under your brand at our plant. You share specs and artwork. We run production, quality checks, labelling, and delivery on agreed volumes. See what we can do for private label and contract manufacturing.

Who can buy from Elegant Enterprises?

We welcome inquiries from bulk buyers throughout Sri Lanka. That includes distributors expanding a hygiene range, supermarkets and pharmacies stocking household essentials, and hotels, restaurants, and offices with steady consumption needs.

Retail shops and resellers may purchase for their local markets. For custom bulk orders, contact us to discuss quantities, packaging, and delivery schedules. Our sales team supports wholesale pricing and long-term supply plans.

What products do we manufacture?

We make paper serviettes, toilet rolls, multifold hand towels, kitchen paper towels, 3-ply disposable face masks, KN95 face masks, and other protective face masks. Each line ships in retail packs or trade cartons from Galle. See the full product catalogue for specs and packs.
