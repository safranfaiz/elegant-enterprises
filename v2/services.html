For trade buyers

Manufacturing & private label — FAQ

Can Elegant Enterprises manufacture tissue or masks under my own brand?
Yes. We offer private label and OEM manufacturing of toilet tissue, serviettes, face masks, baby care and sanitary products at our factory in Galle, Sri Lanka. You supply the brand and we handle production, packing and labelling.
What is the minimum order quantity for private label manufacturing?
Minimum order quantities depend on the product, pack size and packaging. Share your product mix and expected volumes and we will confirm the MOQ, pricing and lead time in your quotation.
Do you supply distributors and wholesalers across Sri Lanka?
Yes. We supply distributors, wholesalers, supermarkets, pharmacies and institutional buyers island-wide, with bulk cartons and pallet-ready dispatch from Galle.
Can I visit the factory before placing an order?
Yes. Trade buyers can visit our manufacturing plant at 496 Akuressa Road, Galle by appointment. Call or WhatsApp 074 100 8160 to arrange a visit.
How do I get a manufacturing quotation?
Send us the products, pack sizes, branding requirements and monthly volumes through the contact page, by email to Sales@elegantenterprises.info, or on WhatsApp at 074 100 8160.
